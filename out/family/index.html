What to do during the first 30 days

1

Before Discharge

  • Review medications with the discharge planner
  • Confirm follow-up appointments
  • Add emergency contacts to Care Circle
  • Invite caregivers and family to the plan
2

Week 1

  • Daily medication check-ins
  • Log meals, mood, and mobility observations
  • Attend follow-up appointments
  • Keep the facility in the loop
3

Weeks 2-4

  • Review progress and adjust the care plan
  • Export notes for the primary care visit
  • Celebrate small wins with the circle
