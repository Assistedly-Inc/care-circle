Or a 38-year-old recovering from a stroke. Or a daughter who needs three weeks of safe respite for her mother before her own surgery. US healthcare is built for two extremes, acute hospital care or permanent long-term care, and the middle is broken everywhere.

Acute hospitals

$3–5K / day

Stays measured in days. Patient is discharged the moment they're medically stable even if they cannot safely go home.

Missing middle

2 weeks – 6 months

No clear facility category, limited insurance benefit, no marketplace. Families or hospitals spend weeks calling, most end up at home, alone, unsafe.

Long term care

12+ months

SNFs and assisted living are built around long-stay geriatric residents. The minority of residents under 65 with complex short-stay needs are routinely declined (Health Affairs, 2022).

This is where my uncle ended up. Millions of American families a year live this exact problem.

BUSINESS MODEL

The routing rule removes confusion and most legal risk. Track A: hospital-referred patients, hospital pays a service fee. Track B: families who come directly and pay privately; the facility pays a placement fee, the same model A Place for Mom uses for private-pay senior living. Facility fees are never taken where a federal program or insurer pays for the stay (Anti-Kickback Statute), or from addiction-treatment providers (EKRA), and never on hospital cases (no double payment). Some states regulate placement or referral agencies; check disclosure and licensing rules in NY and NJ. Insured families who come directly are helped at no charge or routed to health-plan contracts later. $4,500 average fee is an assumption (~40% of an ~$11K first month); validate with facility partners. Health-plan contracts (Year 3+) and reserved-bed programs (Year 4+) are later lines on the hospital side.
